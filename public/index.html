📡 NFC tags on the cards

Optional. An NFC sticker on a card lets a customer tap their phone instead of scanning — it opens the same page as that card's QR. Works on Android phones and iPhone XS or newer.

  1. Buy NTAG213 NFC stickers (about RM1–2 each) and stick one on the back of each card.
  2. On your phone, install the free app NFC Tools.
  3. Tap Copy link under a card above, then in NFC Tools: Write → Add a record → URL/URI, paste, and Write. Hold the phone on that card's sticker.
  4. Test it: tap the card with another phone — it should open that card's page.
  5. Optional: Other → Lock tag in NFC Tools so nobody can overwrite it. (A locked tag can't be changed — if you ever press "New QR", you'd need a new sticker.)
