# How to use Mamak POS

This guide is for everybody who works here. You do not need to know anything
about computers. Every step tells you exactly what to tap.

Words you will see on the screen are written **like this**.

> **The one rule to remember:** the screen never sends food to the kitchen by
> itself. Food is only cooked after somebody taps **Send to kitchen** — and
> when a customer orders from their own phone, only after *they* tap
> **Confirm order**.

> **There is also a Help button inside the POS.** Tap **🛟 Help** in the menu
> down the side (or along the bottom on a phone). It has short versions of
> everything below, a search box, and little animations for the things people
> get stuck on. This document is the long version — print it and leave it by
> the till.

## 1. Opening the POS

Turn on the tablet. Open the browser icon. The POS opens by itself.

If it does not, tap the address bar at the top and type the address your boss
gave you, then press **Go**.

---

## 2. Logging in

1. Tap the **Name** box. Type your name.
2. Tap the **PIN** box. Type your PIN (4 to 8 numbers).
3. Tap **Log In**.

**First time?** The POS asks you to choose your own PIN. Type it twice and tap
**Save new PIN**. Nobody else knows it — not even your boss.

**Wrong PIN?** Try again. After ten wrong tries the POS stops accepting attempts
for ten minutes. Typing it correctly never counts against you.

**To change your PIN later:** tap **Change my PIN** at the top of the screen.

---

## 3. Opening the shift

Do this once, when the shop opens. **Payments do not work until a shift is
open.**

1. Tap **🕐 Shift** at the bottom (or top) of the screen.
2. In **Float (RM)**, type how much cash is in the drawer to start with.
3. Tap **Open Shift**.

---

## 4. Taking an order

1. Tap **🍽 Tables**.
2. Tap the table the customer is sitting at.
3. Tap a category along the top — **Nasi**, **Mee**, **Roti**, **Minuman**…
   Slide the row sideways with your finger to see more.
   **🔥 Popular today** at the top shows what is selling most right now.
4. Tap a dish. It goes straight into the order on the right.
   - Tap **+** to add another of the same thing.
   - Tap **−** to remove one.
   - Tap **✕** to take the line off completely.
   - Tap **📝** to add a note like *kurang pedas* or *tak nak ais*.
     There are ready-made buttons for the common ones.
5. Some dishes ask a question first, for example **Kuah?** or **Extra lauk?**.
   Tap the answers, then tap **Add**.
6. When the order is complete, tap the big orange button:

   **🍳 Send 3 new items to kitchen**

   The button always tells you how many items it is about to send.

The items move up into **✅ Already sent**. The kitchen now has them.

---

## 5. Adding more food later — the most important page

This is the thing that used to go wrong. Read it once.

A table has **one bill** but can send food to the kitchen **many times**.
Each time you tap **Send to kitchen**, that batch is called a **round**.

**Example — Table 1**

At 8:01 PM the customer orders:

- Mee Goreng
- Teh O Ais

You tap **🍳 Send 2 new items to kitchen**. That is **Round 1**.

The kitchen cooks it, and by 8:15 PM Round 1 is **Served**.

At 8:17 PM the same customer asks for **Roti Canai**.

1. Tap **🍽 Tables** → **Table 1**.
2. Tap **Roti** → **Roti Canai**.
3. Tap **🍳 Send 1 new item to kitchen**.

Now the bill looks like this:

```
✅ ALREADY SENT

Round 1                              🍽 Served
  1× Mee Goreng                      RM  8.50
  1× Teh O Ais                       RM  2.50

Round 2                           🔔 New order
  1× Roti Canai                      RM  2.00
```

- It is still **one bill**. The customer pays once, at the end, for everything.
- **Round 2 is new.** It is not served. The kitchen has just received it.
- **Round 1 stays served.** Nothing about it changed.
- The kitchen printer prints **only the Roti Canai**, with **ADD-ON · ROUND 2**
  at the top and your name on it. It does not print the whole order again.

You can do this as many times as the customer wants.

To see the whole history, tap **Order timeline** at the bottom of the bill.

---

## 6. The kitchen screen

Tap **🍳 Kitchen**. There are four columns:

| Column | What it means |
|---|---|
| **🔔 New** | Just arrived. Nobody has started it. |
| **🍳 Cooking** | Someone is making it now. |
| **✅ Ready** | Finished, waiting to be carried out. |
| **🍽 Served** | Already on the table. |

Each card shows the table, the time it was sent, **who sent it**, and how many
minutes it has been waiting.

**Each card has one button — the next thing to do.**

- A card in **New** says **🍳 Start cooking**.
- A card in **Cooking** says **✅ Ready**.
- A card in **Ready** says **🍽 Served**.

Tap it, and the card moves to the next column.

**Tapped the wrong card?** Tap **↶ Undo** underneath it, within a few seconds.
(Undo is for waiters and the boss. If you are logged in as kitchen staff, ask a
waiter.)

**A card marked ADD-ON · ROUND 2** is extra food for a table that already ate.
It is a new card. Cook it now.

**⏱ minutes** on each card: under 5 is fine, 5–10 needs attention, over 10 is
late and turns red.

---

## 7. The drinks station

If your shop has a drinks station, there are buttons above the columns:
**Kitchen** and **Drinks**. Tap **Drinks** to see only drinks.

One table's round can have food *and* drinks. The kitchen card shows only the
food, the drinks card shows only the drinks, and each is marked ready on its
own. The customer still gets one bill.

---

## 8. Ready and served

When the food is ready, the kitchen taps **✅ Ready**.

Carry it out, then tap **🍽 Served** on that card.

On the **🍽 Tables** screen the table tile now says **💵 Ready to pay**.

---

## 9. Taking payment

1. Tap **🍽 Tables** → the table.
2. Tap the green **💵 Pay** button.
3. The screen shows the total and **To pay**.
4. Tap the way the customer is paying:
   - **💵 Cash** — if you want the change worked out for you, type what the
     customer handed you into **Cash received (RM)** first. The screen shows
     **Change: RM …**
   - **💳 Card**
   - **📱 DuitNow / eWallet**

The receipt prints and the table goes back to being free.

**Paying part now, part later?** Under **Pay a specific amount (RM)**, type the
amount and tap **Apply cash** (or card, or eWallet). The screen shows what is
still owed. Repeat until it reaches zero.

**Discount?** Tap **Discount**, choose percent or amount, and type the reason.
If you are not the boss, an admin has to type their name and PIN to approve it.

---

## 10. Splitting a bill

In the payment screen:

- **Split evenly** — type how many people, tap **Split**. Each share appears
  with its own **Pay cash** / **Pay card** button. Tap one as each person pays.
- **Split by seat** — only works if you gave each line a seat number while
  taking the order (the small **Seat** box under each new item).

---

## 11. Voiding an item (taking it off the bill)

1. Open the table.
2. Find the line under **✅ Already sent**.
3. Tap **❌ Void**.
4. Type why — for example *customer changed their mind*. This is recorded.
5. Tap **Void it**.

The kitchen gets a **VOID** slip so they stop cooking it, and the item comes off
the bill.

**Rules:**
- If the kitchen has not started that item yet, any waiter can void it.
- Once the kitchen has started it, only the boss can.
- A round that is still new can always be voided by a waiter, **even if an
  earlier round was already served**.
- You cannot void anything after the bill is paid. That needs a refund.

---

## 12. Sold out

When something runs out:

1. Tap **⚙ Admin** → **🍜 Menu**.
2. Find the dish (use the search box).
3. Tap the big green **✅ Available** button. It turns red: **🚫 Sold out today**.

It disappears from the till and from the customer QR menu straight away.

**It comes back by itself at midnight.** You do not have to remember.

To take something off the menu *permanently*, tap **✏️ Edit** and turn off
**On the menu**. That stays off until you turn it back on.

---

## 13. Takeaway

Takeaway is not a table.

1. Tap **🍽 Tables**.
2. Under **🥡 Takeaway**, tap **➕ New Takeaway**.
3. Add the food the same way as a table order.
4. Tap **🍳 Send … to kitchen**.

It gets a number, like **Takeaway #128**, and appears in the **🥡 Takeaway**
section. The kitchen card says **Takeaway #128** instead of a table.

You can have as many takeaway orders open at once as you like.

---

## 14. Moving a table

The customer moved from Table 3 to Table 5:

1. Open **Table 3**.
2. Tap **↔ Move** at the top.
3. Choose **Table 5**.
4. Tap **Move order**.

Everything moves — the food, the rounds, the bill. **Do not re-enter the
order.** The kitchen keeps cooking.

You can only move to a table that is free.

---

## 15. Customers ordering with the QR code

Every table has a QR sticker. The customer points their phone camera at it and
the menu opens. No app to install.

- They choose food and tap **Place Order**.
- The kitchen gets it immediately, marked **QR**.
- Their phone shows **Sent → Cooking → Ready → Served** as it happens.
- They can tap **Order More** and order again. It goes on the **same bill**, as
  a new round — exactly like when you add food for them.

**You still take the payment at the counter as normal.**

If the boss has turned on approval, QR orders wait for you first. A blue box
appears at the top of the **🍳 Kitchen** screen:

> ⏳ Customer orders waiting for you
> **Table 4** · 1× Nasi Goreng · [✅ Accept & send] [❌ Reject]

Tap **Accept & send** and it goes to the kitchen. Tap **Reject** and you have to
say why — the customer sees it, and the item is taken off their bill.

---

## 15b. Customers ordering by speaking

Where the owner has switched it on, the page a customer sees after scanning
leads with a big microphone: **🎙 Speak to Order**.

They tap it and talk the way they would talk to you:

> *"Roti canai dua, teh tarik satu kurang manis."*

English, Bahasa Malaysia, or both mixed together — it does not need proper
grammar.

**What the customer sees next**

The POS shows them what it understood:

```
HERE'S WHAT I GOT

2× Roti Canai                          RM 4.00
1× Teh Tarik                           RM 2.80
   kurang manis

Total                                  RM 6.80
```

They can change the numbers, remove a line, add more from the menu, or say a
correction out loud — *"make the teh tarik two"*, *"cancel the roti"*. Nothing
reaches you until they tap **Confirm order**.

**What you need to know**

- It arrives as an ordinary **QR** round on the kitchen screen. Same tickets,
  same rounds, same bill. There is nothing new to learn.
- **The prices are always this restaurant's prices.** The computer is never
  allowed to invent one — it can only say *which dish*, and the till looks up
  what that dish costs.
- If something is sold out, it is refused and the customer is told, exactly as
  if they had tapped it.
- If a dish needs an answer — *Kuah?* — the customer is asked before they can
  confirm.
- If it mishears, they will see it on that preview and fix it. If it keeps
  mishearing, just take the order yourself. It is an extra way to order, never
  the only one.

---

## 16. Pausing QR ordering

Boss only. If the kitchen is overwhelmed:

1. Tap **⚙ Admin** → **🍽 Tables & QR**.
2. Turn **Accept QR orders** off.

Customers scanning the QR now see:

> Online ordering is temporarily paused. Please order with our staff.

Turn it back on the same way.

In the same place, **QR handling** chooses between:
- **Send directly to kitchen** (normal, and the default)
- **Require staff approval** (every QR order waits for a staff member)

## 17. Adding and editing the menu

Boss only. Tap **⚙ Admin** → **🍜 Menu**.

**To add a dish:**
1. Tap **➕ Add item**.
2. **Item name** — what staff and customers call it.
3. **Category** — which tab it appears under.
4. **Price (RM)**.
5. **Prepared at** — **Kitchen** or **Drinks**. This decides which screen and
   which printer it goes to.
6. Tap **Save**.

**To change a dish:** tap **✏️ Edit** next to it, change what you need, tap
**Save**.

**To delete a dish:** tap **✏️ Edit**, then **Delete this item**.
Old bills keep their own copy of the name and price, so nothing in your records
changes. If the dish is on a table's open order right now, the POS refuses and
tells you — finish that order first.

**To add a category:** tap **➕ Category** and type the name. Use **▲** and
**▼** to change the order the tabs appear in.

## 18. Food options

A food option group is a **question the till asks**, and its options are the
**answers**. For example:

> **Extra Lauk** — choose several, up to 3
> Chicken +RM 3.00 · Mutton +RM 4.00 · Egg +RM 1.50

**⚙ Admin** → **🍜 Menu** → **Food options**.

- **➕ Add option group** — name it, choose **One only** or **Several**, and set
  the minimum and maximum.
- **✏️ Edit** on a group — add options, rename them, change their price, mark
  one **Sold out**, reorder them with **▲ ▼**, or delete one.
- **Duplicate** copies a whole group with its options, for when you want a
  second version.
- Each group shows **Used by:** the dishes that ask it.

**To attach a group to a dish:** **✏️ Edit** the dish and tap the option group
chips at the bottom. A chip with **✓** is attached.

Deleting a group that dishes still use asks you to confirm and lists them. Old
bills are never changed.

---

## 19. Adding staff and resetting PINs

Boss only. **⚙ Admin** → **👤 Staff**.

**To add someone:** type their **Name**, choose **Role**, type a temporary
**PIN** twice, tap **Add staff**. They choose their own PIN when they first
log in.

The roles are:
- **Staff** — tables, kitchen, shift, sales.
- **Kitchen** — the kitchen screen only.
- **Admin** — everything, including this page.

**To reset a forgotten PIN:** tap **Reset PIN** next to their name and give
them a new temporary one. They will be asked to choose their own.

**Somebody left:** tap **Deactivate**. They are signed out immediately and
cannot log in. They are **not deleted** — old bills still have their name on
them, and that has to stay true.

---

## 20. Printing

Printing is automatic:

- **Kitchen chit** — when you tap **Send to kitchen**. One per round, per
  station. An add-on prints only its own items.
- **Void slip** — when you void a line.
- **Receipt** — when a bill is paid in full.

The boss sets printers up under **⚙ Admin** → **🖨 Printers**. **Test print**
next to a printer prints a test slip so you can check the paper and the cable.

---

## 21. Retrying a failed print

Paper ran out, or the printer was switched off, and nothing came out.

1. **⚙ Admin** → **🖨 Printers** → **Print jobs**.
2. Look for a red **🔴** line, for example:

   > 🔴 Kitchen chit — **Table 3 · Round 2 · Kitchen** — 8:42 PM

3. Fix the printer (paper, power, cable).
4. Tap **🖨 Retry**.

It prints **exactly the same ticket again**. It does **not** create another
order, another round, or another charge, and it does not change the bill.
Every retry is recorded.

## 22. System status

**⚙ Admin** → **⚙ System**. Everything here is checked at the moment you open
it — nothing shows green just because it exists.

| Row | 🟢 means | 🔴 means |
|---|---|---|
| **Database** | The POS can save orders | Call for help immediately |
| **Live updates** | Screens are updating each other | — |
| **Kitchen** | Nothing is running late | Something has waited over 10 minutes |
| **Each printer** | It answered just now | It is off, unplugged, or on the wrong address |
| **QR public address** | Printed QR codes work | Customers' phones cannot open them |
| **QR ordering** | Customers can order | Paused |
| **Last backup** | A backup ran recently | No backup has ever reported in |
| **Off-device backup** | Backups also go somewhere else | They only exist on this machine |
| **Disk space** | There is room | The machine is nearly full |

Tap **↻ Check now** to check again.

## 23. Closing the shift

At the end of the day:

1. Take payment for every table first. The **🍽 Tables** screen should show no
   open bills.
2. Tap **🕐 Shift** → **Close Shift**.
3. Count the drawer. Type how many of each note and coin you have. The POS adds
   it up for you.
4. If the total does not match what the POS expected, type a note explaining
   why. This is required.
5. Tap **Confirm Close**.

The Z report appears. Tap **Print** for a paper copy, or **Export CSV**.

---

## 24. The Sales screen

Tap **💰 Sales**. At a glance:

- **Today sales** — and whether that is up or down on all of yesterday
- **Orders** and **Average order**
- **Open tables** and how much money is sitting on the floor
- **Ready to pay** — tables to go and collect from
- **Late in kitchen** — anything over 10 minutes
- **This month** and **This year**

Below that: sales by hour, top items today, how people are paying, and what the
kitchen is doing right now, plus today's voids, discounts and refunds.

## 25. When something goes wrong

### The internet is down

**Keep taking orders.** The POS remembers them.

- A red bar appears: **Offline — 2 orders waiting to send**.
- Items show **⏳ sending**.
- When the internet comes back, everything sends by itself. Nothing is lost and
  nothing is sent twice.

**You cannot take payment while offline.** Write the payment on paper, and enter
it as soon as the internet returns — before closing the shift.

### The kitchen printer is not printing

1. Check the paper and the power.
2. **⚙ Admin** → **⚙ System** to see whether the printer answers.
3. Fix it, then **⚙ Admin** → **🖨 Printers** → **Print jobs** → **🖨 Retry** on
   each red line.

Meanwhile, the **🍳 Kitchen** screen has everything. **The kitchen never
depends on the printer.**

### A customer says the QR code does not work

1. **⚙ Admin** → **🍽 Tables & QR**. If there is a red box at the top, the
   address is wrong — your boss or the person who set the POS up has to fix
   **BASE_URL** (see `docs/RUNBOOK.md`).
2. Check **Accept QR orders** is on.
3. Tap **Open** under the table to test the link yourself.
4. If the sticker is damaged, tap **Print** to print a new one.

Meanwhile, just take the order for them.

### Common problems

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Session expired** | Log in again. Nothing was lost. |
| **Cannot take payment while offline** | Wait for the internet. Write it on paper. |
| **No shift is open** | **🕐 Shift** → **Open Shift**. |
| **Order has a payment recorded; cannot add items** | The bill is being settled. Start a new order, or refund first. |
| **Table already has an open order** | Someone else opened it. Tap that table to see it. |
| **Admin only once the kitchen has started this item** | Ask the boss to void it. |
| **This QR code is not in use** | The table was retired. Take the order yourself. |
| Screen is stuck | Pull down to refresh the page. Nothing is lost. |

## 26. Help inside the POS

Tap **🛟 Help**. It is the last item in the menu down the left (or along the
bottom on a phone), and everyone can see it.

- **Search** — type any word. "sold out", "add more", "printer", "split".
- **Topics** — short cards, each one about a thing that goes wrong rather than
  a screen that exists. Each says roughly how long it takes to read.
- **Little animations** — six of the topics show you the taps: taking an
  order, adding more food later, the kitchen screen, taking payment, marking
  something sold out, and how Speak to Order works. They play by themselves,
  and you can pause or step through them.
- **Common questions** — the nine things people actually ask, in plain
  language, each linking to the full topic.

Some screens also have a small **?** link — on Kitchen, on the menu editor, on
the QR settings — that opens the matching topic directly.

# QUICK START — STAFF

*Print this page and stick it by the till.*

### Every day

**Open shift** → 🕐 Shift → type the float → **Open Shift**

### Take an order

🍽 Tables → tap the table → tap a category → tap the dishes →
**🍳 Send N new items to kitchen**

### Add more food later

🍽 Tables → tap the **same table** → tap the dishes →
**🍳 Send N new items to kitchen**

> It becomes **Round 2**. Same bill. The kitchen gets a new card. The earlier
> food stays served. **Never start a new table for the same customer.**

### Kitchen

🍳 Kitchen → each card has one button → tap it

**🍳 Start cooking** → **✅ Ready** → **🍽 Served**

Wrong tap? **↶ Undo** straight away.

### Take payment

🍽 Tables → tap the table → **💵 Pay** → tap **Cash**, **Card** or **DuitNow**

Cash: type what they gave you first, and the change is worked out for you.

### Something off the bill

Open the table → find the line → **❌ Void** → type why → **Void it**

### Sold out

⚙ Admin → 🍜 Menu → find it → tap **✅ Available** → it becomes **🚫 Sold out
today** → it comes back by itself tomorrow

### Takeaway

🍽 Tables → **➕ New Takeaway** → add food → **🍳 Send to kitchen**

### End of day

Pay off every table → 🕐 Shift → **Close Shift** → count the drawer →
**Confirm Close**

### Customer speaks their order

They tap 🎙 Speak to Order on their phone, talk, check what came back, tap
**Confirm order**. It lands on your kitchen screen as a QR round. Nothing
reaches you before they confirm.

### Stuck

Tap **🛟 Help** and type what you are trying to do.

### Internet down

Keep taking orders — they send by themselves later.
**Payments must wait.** Write them on paper.
