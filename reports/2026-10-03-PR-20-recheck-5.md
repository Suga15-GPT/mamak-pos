## Method

- Database and setup:
  - Only the throwaway Postgres at localhost:5434. Every script refuses any other address.
  - The review folder was fast-forwarded to 49d2a26 (`git merge --ff-only`) and ran it.
  - Controls on ff92e19 and main (d947fab) came from `git archive` in my scratch folder.
  - Every schema came fresh from its tree's migrations. Gemini was a local stand-in.
- Node: 24.19 for everything, and 20.20.2 (the official Windows zip, checksum checked, kept in my scratch folder) for
  `npm test`'s files, `wae_app.js`, `wae_multi.js` and the unit-test controls and probes. The shop's Docker image
  (Node 20 on Alpine Linux) was not run.
- Time zones: everything ran in Malaysia time, as docker-compose runs the app. Nothing in this commit reads a date or
  a clock time (the boot id uses the clock only to be unique), so I did not repeat the runs in UTC.
- Builds: each build ran as its own server process on one port and one schema; a restart or an update stops one and
  starts the next on the same port and schema. Service workers on, each device its own Chromium profile. Chromium
  from Playwright 1.62.1. Connections were counted with `netstat`, with no proxy.
- `STREAM_MAX_MS`: the default (5 minutes) for the restarts, the walks and the soak; 20 s, 15 s, 12 s, 8 s, 6 s, 3 s
  and 2 s where said.
- Real taps: Chromium mouse clicks at one point (`restart_pay.js`; `f2_realtap.js` with second taps 0–1,000 ms after
  the first).
- Races: `restart_pay.js` 40 runs, every ordering of the add-on, the reconnect and the tap; `ui20_pay.js` 40 per
  case; `ui_stalepay.js` 60.
- Scripts re-run from earlier reports: `wae_app.js` (with `stall_client.py`), `gap_zero.js`, `stream_end.js`,
  `stream_check.js`, `sw_stream_cache.js`, `n2_reload5.js`, `t7_conn5.js`, `ui20_pay.js`, `ui_stalepay.js` and
  `f2_realtap.js` (SHARE at 1280×800).
- New scripts: `wae_multi.js`, `restart_screens.js`, `restart_pay.js`, `restart_menu.js`, `since_probe.js`,
  `conn_trend.js`, and the unit-test probes `stream_probe.test.js` (the new test with a size knob),
  `f1_unit_probe.test.js`, `f1_cork_probe.test.js` and `f1_stub_probe.test.js`.
- Not re-run, because this commit does not touch that code: the expense, sales, tile, split, combine and Clear sales
  data scripts, the upgrade, root and layout checks, `offline_probe.js` (`stream_check.js` covers the offline queue),
  and the PR #19 scripts.
- Housekeeping: every script's schemas were dropped afterwards. The live install, its containers, its `.env` and
  docker compose were not touched.
