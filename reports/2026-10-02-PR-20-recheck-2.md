## Findings, most severe first

### F1. On a phone, the "POS version" line the RUNBOOK asks for is hidden under the bottom bar (Medium; new)
- What goes wrong:
  - e8e7222 puts `POS version …` at the very end of 🛟 Help (public/index.html:415-416, public/style.css:968).
  - Below 768 px the menu is a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen. The rule that keeps the page clear of it
    (public/style.css:516, `main { padding-bottom:calc(var(--bottomnav-h) + 24px + …) }`) is overridden by
    `main { padding:22px }` (style.css:519) and, at 600 px and less, `main { padding:14px }` (style.css:521).
  - So the last ~50 px of the page stay under the bar, however far you scroll, and the version line is exactly there.
- Reproduction (`help_label.js`, `help_bottom.js`; Chromium, Help scrolled to the very end):

  | Screen | "POS version e6fe370df9f0" |
  |---|---|
  | 1280×800 | on screen |
  | 800×1280 | on screen |
  | 390×844 | under the bar (45 px below its top edge), not visible |
  | 360×640 | under the bar, not visible |

  - The page's bottom padding at 390 px is 14 px instead of 90 px.
  - Control: d5a9b4b and main have no version line. On both, the last 53 px of Help are already under the bar at
    390×844 and 360×640, so the padding problem is old. The line that matters is new.
- What it costs the shop:
  - The handbook runs the POS on phones ("along the bottom on a phone"). The steps do update a phone: at 390×844 all
    five one-tab tills reached e8e7222 after two close-and-opens (`n2_reload3.js` VIEWPORT=390x844). But step 3
    can't pass there, so "close and open once more" never ends.
  - A phone still on main's screens and a phone on the new build look the same at the foot of Help. The PR's "Each
    till shows its build at the foot of 🛟 Help" doesn't hold on a phone.
  - The padding is the same on every screen, so other screens' last lines may hide too; I measured Help only.
- Suggested fix: show the version where it can be seen (under the Help title, say), or let the bottom-bar padding
  win (move style.css:516 after 519–521, or give it more weight). Check it at 390×844.

## Method

- Database and setup:
  - Only the throwaway Postgres at localhost:5434. Every script refuses any other address.
  - The review folder stayed at d5a9b4b: its fast-forward was not allowed in this session. e8e7222 ran from
    `git archive` in my scratch folder, as did the controls on d5a9b4b and d947fab. Every schema came fresh from
    that tree's migrations, with the new 021.
  - Gemini was a local stand-in.

  Record's taps ran again in UTC.
- Real taps: Chromium mouse clicks at one point, with gaps of 0–1000 ms, at 1280×800, 800×1280 and 390×844.
- Builds: each build ran as its own server process on one port and one schema, with service workers on, each till
  in its own browser.
- Races: 40 runs each (200 for E7), every ordering of the two sides.
- Scripts re-run from earlier reports: `exp_checks.js`, `edge_checks.js`, `gemini_check.js`, `root_check.js`,
  `prev_check.js`, `upgrade_021.js`, `explorer_z2.js`, `split_fuzz.js`, `n3_races.js`, `clear_races.js`,

  `n2_reload2.js` (as `n2_reload3.js`).
