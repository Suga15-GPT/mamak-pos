## Findings, most severe first

### F1. The shop's first update to this PR does not reach the tills with "one manual reload" (Medium; new — the claim and the instructions)
- What goes wrong:
  - The PR description (N2) and docs/RUNBOOK.md:362-367 say the first update that adds the automatic reload
    "needs one manual reload of every till".
  - A till on main is run by main's service worker (public/sw.js on d947fab, `v9`). That worker answers pages from
    its cache and carries the till's live stream (`/api/stream`), so it is always busy.
  - After the update:
    - the 1st reload is answered from main's cache, and the new worker installs and waits;
    - the 2nd reload may let the new worker take over, but the page is main's again;
    - only a later load shows the new build.
  - A tab opened after the update stays on main's code, with the new worker waiting.
  - Main's screens have no `version.js`, so a till left like this never updates itself.
- Reproduction (`n2_reload2.js` MODE=MAIN): main (d947fab) swapped for d5a9b4b on one port and database, each till
  in its own browser, Malaysia time.

  | Run | What was done to two tills open through the update | Result |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | Reloaded by hand at 40 s and 71 s | both still on main at 96 s |
  | 2 | Reloaded at 40 s and 66 s, then the tab closed and opened again at 91 s | both on the new build from 96 s |
  | 3 | Three reloads (41, 61, 81 s) | 1 of 2 on the new build from 86 s; the other on main at 116 s |
  | 4 | The tab closed and opened again at 40 s and at 66 s, no reloads | both on the new build from 73 s |

  Two tills first opened after the update stayed on main, with the new worker waiting, for 96 s (run 1) and 140 s
  (run 2).
- What it costs the shop: after following the RUNBOOK, every till still runs main's screens.
  - No Expenses and no explorer on them, and no automatic reload from then on.
  - Main's double-tap bugs, which this PR fixes, stay live. A double tap on a share pays the rest by e-wallet:
    main 50 of 50 at 40–400 ms in the last review; 1c85f11 53 of 54 at 40–600 ms in this one.
  - Nothing on the screen says which build a till runs.
- Suggested fix:
  - For this update, tell the shop: on every till, close the POS tab (or the app) and open it again, twice.
    Then check that the new screens are there (the owner's 🧾 Expenses). Run 4 is that procedure.
  - Correct the PR description and RUNBOOK.md.
  - Better still, show the build somewhere staff can read it (Help or the menu).

### F2. Saving a day-old photo draft can still lose the photo; on a shop with years of receipts it usually does (Low; not new — F5 narrowed, not fixed)
- What goes wrong (src/services/expenses.js:472-476, the clean-up in `extract()`; migrations/021_expenses.sql:60,
  `ON DELETE SET NULL`):
  - The clean-up checks "no expense uses this photo" against the database as it was when its DELETE started.
  - A save that commits while that DELETE is still running has already let go of its `FOR KEY SHARE`, so
    `SKIP LOCKED` has nothing to skip.
  - The photo is deleted, and `ON DELETE SET NULL` takes it off the expense that was just saved.
  - The clean-up looks at every saved expense, so the more receipts a shop keeps, the longer it runs and the
    wider the gap.
- Reproduction:
  - `exp_checks.js` E7 (fresh database, Malaysia time, 200 runs): 1 lost the photo, 80 were refused with "no longer
    here", 119 kept it, no server errors. 1c85f11: 12 lost and 42 server errors. In UTC, 40 runs: 1 lost.
  - `f5_more.js` LONG, 40 runs per size, with N saved expenses that have photos:

    | Saved expenses with photos (N) | Saves that lost the photo, of 40 |
    |---|---|
    | 1,000 | 0 |
    | 3,000 | 1 |
    | 10,000 | 5 |
    | 20,000 | 26 (1c85f11: 29) |

  - `f5_mech.js`, deterministic: the clean-up statement slowed with `pg_sleep`; a save that held the photo and
    committed 314 ms after the clean-up started. Result: the expense's photo is gone (`receipt_id` NULL).
  - The same with the check made again after the lock, in a second statement: the photo is kept, and the old
    unsaved photo is still removed.
- What it costs the shop: an expense saved without its receipt photo, with no message. The amount is right; the
  receipt the accountant asks for is gone. It needs a draft open for over a day and another photo read at the
  same moment.
- Suggested fix:
  - In one transaction, `SELECT id … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` the candidates, then
    `DELETE … WHERE id = ANY($1) AND NOT EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM expenses e WHERE e.receipt_id = r.id)`. The second
    statement sees saves that committed meanwhile.
  - Or make the foreign key `ON DELETE RESTRICT`, so the delete fails instead of emptying the photo.
  - Either way, an index on `expenses(receipt_id)` keeps the clean-up short.
