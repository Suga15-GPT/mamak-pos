## Findings, most severe first

### F1. Regular costs break on a server in Malaysia time (High; new)
- shapeRecurring turns starts_on and last_done_for into text with
  toISOString() (src/services/expenses.js:204-209). node-postgres reads a DATE
  as local midnight, which in KL is 16:00 UTC the day before, so both dates
  come out a day early. due() and settleDue() count from a day too soon.
  docker-compose.yml runs the app with TZ=Asia/Kuala_Lumpur.
- Reproduction (exp_checks.js, KL): a monthly cost started 70 days ago is due
  23 Jul. Record → 200, and 23 Jul is still due. Record again → 500
  (expenses_one_per_due). Skip → 200, still 23 Jul. A cost starting today can
  fall due yesterday. In the till, Record shows "Recorded" and the row stays,
  40 of 40. The PR's own unit test (expenses.test.js:143) and e2e journey
  (journeys.spec.js:828) fail in KL; both pass with TZ=UTC.
- Fix: select the dates as text, or a pg type parser for DATE (OID 1082); run
  the expenses tests with TZ=Asia/Kuala_Lumpur.

### F2. A double tap on one person's share of an even split settles the rest of the bill as e-wallet (High; not new, but N1 says it is fixed)
- paySplitShare holds payBusy only until its request and the redraw finish
  (public/js/pos.js:1084-1092), ~10–30 ms. The redraw adds "Paid so far" above
  the buttons and moves 📱 DuitNow / eWallet (pay the rest) under the finger
  (checked at 1280×800, 800×1280, 390×844). The second tap pays the rest with
  the new total as expected_due, so it is accepted; the bill closes as paid.
- Reproduction (n1_realtap.js): RM 9.01 split 3 ways, share 1 "Pay card"
  tapped twice at one point. 0–20 ms: one payment (PR), two on main. 40–400
  ms: Card 3.01 + eWallet 6.00 in 49 of 50 (PR), 50 of 50 (main).
- Cost: RM 6.00 of e-wallet recorded that nobody paid; the bill closes.
- Fix: hold the guard ~500 ms after the redraw, or keep the share list still,
  or ignore pay-in-full taps for a moment after a payment.

### F3. Tills don't reliably move to a new build (N2) (Medium; new code)
- The till's live updates (EventSource('/api/stream')) go through the service
  worker's networkFirst (public/sw.js:65), so the old worker always has a fetch
  in flight; the new worker waits. version.js gives up after 8 s and reloads;
  the old worker answers from the old cache.
- / carries no build stamp (express.static answers it; only /index.html is
  stamped). A page without a stamp takes the server's version as its own and
  never reloads again.
- Reproduction (n2_reload.js, n2_diag.js): two builds swapped under five
  tills; T1 at / reloaded into the old build and stayed old; T5 (unsent line)
  reloaded after Send into the old build.
- Fix: let /api/stream bypass the service worker; reload only once the new
  worker is active; stamp / too; never adopt the server's version when the page
  has no stamp; until then, keep "reload every till after an update".

### F4. The Sales screen shows two different "today" figures (Low–Medium; tiles not new)
- The tiles count status = 'paid' only (src/routes/reports.js:66-78): a fully
  refunded bill drops out, a partial refund isn't taken off. The explorer below
  uses the Z report's meaning. Three RM 9.01 bills, one refunded RM 2.00 and
  one in full: tile RM 18.02; explorer sales 27.03, refunds 11.01, net 16.02;
  X report 27.03 / 11.01.
- Fix: give the tiles the explorer's meaning, or say what they count.

### F5. Saving a draft whose photo is over a day old can fail or lose the photo (Low; new)
- create() checks the receipt exists, then inserts (expenses.js:97-104); each
  new photo read deletes unsaved receipts over a day old (:435). E7, 40 runs:
  10 runs 500 on expenses_receipt_id_fkey; 2 runs the saved expense lost its
  photo (ON DELETE SET NULL).
- Fix: check the receipt FOR KEY SHARE in the insert's transaction, or answer
  the foreign-key error with the existing sentence.

### F6. A double tap on "Record" files two due dates (Low; new)
- With two months due, the first tap redraws with the next month's Record in
  the same place; the second tap records it at the template amount, unchecked:
  33 of 33 at 30–250 ms (UTC).
- Fix: the same as F2, a short guard after the redraw.

### F7. "A day's explorer figures equal that day's Z report" has two exceptions (Low; claim and docs)
- A shift past midnight is split across two days (shift 22:15–07:15: Z RM
  801.25 / 100.20 / 29; Today 422.18 / 50.22 / 16; Yesterday 379.07 / 49.98 /
  13; the two days together equal the Z).
- The Z report's category lines count price × qty without options
  (src/services/shifts.js:185); they fall short of its gross on 40 of 40 days.
- Fix: say "for a shift that opens and closes on the same day"; add option
  prices to the Z report's categories.

### F8. The test claims don't hold on this PC (Low; tests)
- npm test 257/260: two customer_split tests fail on Windows —

  the expenses race test fails (F1). In UTC: 258/260.

- One receipt photo can be attached to two expenses.
